How the batches and modules work

The syllabus is split into self-contained modules, and admissions roll. You do not have to wait for the next annual cohort or join halfway through something already running — you start at the beginning of the next module opening, and pick up the earlier ones as they come round again within your cycle.

It is built this way because almost nobody preparing for this exam has a clear calendar. A module you miss to a posting, a night rota or a personal month is a module you catch, not a course you have fallen out of.

Not sure which fits, or when the next module opens? Send your sitting date and where you are in training, and you will get a straight answer about which programme you need — including if the answer is none of them yet.
