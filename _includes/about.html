I am a practising consultant anaesthesiologist and intensivist in Pune. I teach EDAIC because I sat it, and because almost everything available to candidates in this part of the world is either a question dump or a lecture series that ignores how the paper is built.

What Creston teaches is not a library of recordings. The programme runs in batches, through modules built around how the paper is actually constructed, so that candidates work through the syllabus together, against the format, in a fixed order and to a fixed pace.
