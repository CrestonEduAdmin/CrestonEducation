The diploma at a glance ESAIC
Part I
Two written papers — basic sciences, then clinical anaesthesia and intensive care.
Part II
Four structured oral examinations in a single day, taken once you hold specialist certification.
Who can sit it
Any doctor with a primary medical qualification who is in or through anaesthesia training. No European post and no prior European experience required.
Where it counts
The pan-European standard for the specialty, and accepted for exemption from the RCoA and CAI primary examinations.
Held
Annually in September, in several languages, at centres worldwide.
